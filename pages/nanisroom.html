Nani's Room x Somerset House

About the Project

"Nani's Room" is a game exploring the themes of home, personal objects, memory, and identity. Taking its name and inspiration from my grandmother's room, the project invites players to rediscover a space through the objects that fill it — each one holding a fragment of memory, history, and meaning. Through interaction with these everyday belongings, the game asks what it means to carry a sense of home and identity across time, distance, and generations.

Players explore the room at their own pace, piecing together memories through the objects they find. The experience is designed to be accessible and intuitive, so it can be picked up by audiences of all ages and backgrounds.

Somerset House: Future Artists Programme

Nani's Room was developed and exhibited in connection with Somerset House's Future Artists Programme, a development opportunity for young and emerging creatives run in collaboration with Somerset House Studios' resident artists. The programme combines artist-led workshops with immersion in Somerset House's wider creative community, offering hands-on support in developing new work, experimenting across disciplines, and preparing for a career in the arts. Being part of this environment shaped how Nani's Room evolved — from an early concept about memory and objects into an interactive piece shared with a public audience.
