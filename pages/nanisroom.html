Nani's Room

A memory game about home, personal objects and identity, inspired by my grandmother's room.

Play the game ↗GitHub ↗
01

About the project

What does home feel like to you? Is it a house? A person? Certain objects, sounds, or music? Or is it the way all of these things come together to remind you of somewhere, or someone, you belong to?

Nani's Room is an immersive digital space that explores memory, nostalgia, and the meaning of home within a British Asian experience. Each object in the room holds a memory.

It was made for the Future Artists Programme at Somerset House and curated for the programme's Work in Process showcase.

02

How it works

  1. Enter the room. Best experienced with sound on.
  2. Start with the record player. It plays a traditional song to guide you around the room.
  3. Drag to look around and click the glowing keepsakes to open each memory.
  4. Find all 8. Once every keepsake is found, the room slowly empties and the light goes out.
  5. Reflect. A final question asks where the memories go once the objects are gone.

There is also a day and night mode that changes the light in the room.

The keepsakes

Catching the light

On the wall

The Painting

Was it a picture with meaning? Or was it just on sale? Eother way it always something to look at.

Every occasion, filed away

The Photo Albums

A whole stack of them, spines cracked from being pulled out again and again.

Before stepping out

The Makeup Box

An eyeliner, and a few dried out lipsticks. She knew what she liked but the glam always made an appearance on her best days

Sunday mornings

The Oil

The smell alone brings it back. Hair, face, shoulders. The ritual was always carried out on visits.
